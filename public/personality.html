Personality profile

Build a dossier from a document

Drop a journal, letter, transcript, PDF, or Word file. The page reads what it can in the browser, then sends the file to POST /personality_profile so the server can extract the rest, analyze the person, and save the profile.

Drop the full document here or click to browse — txt, md, pdf, docx, html, json, csv, rtf. Images/audio need a transcript.

    Upload only documents you have the right to use. Profiles recreate a persona from text; they are not a clinical record. Do not upload someone else’s private writing without permission. Stored sources and memories remain until you delete the profile.
